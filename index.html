Ball-and-Beam PID Controller

Embedded hardware · Real-time control

A physical real-time control system designed and built from scratch: a time-of-flight sensor measures ball position, a PID loop computes the correction, and a servo tilts the beam to hold the setpoint. Gains are tuned live from an onboard joystick with an OLED dashboard — no computer required.

No. 03

Statically Balanced Mechanism

Mechanism design · Gravity compensation

A 3D-printed arm that holds its payload perfectly still at any angle — passively, with no motor and zero holding torque. A linear spring, routed by a string over an analytically derived teardrop cam, produces a torque that cancels gravity identically across a full rotation. The cam profile falls out of one equation: constant total potential energy, ∂V/∂θ = 0.

Cam profileR(θ) = Rmax cos(θ/2)
Cam radius Rmax = √(mgL/k)0.980 in
Payload / arm0.485 kg on a 5 in arm
Holding torquezero, full 360°

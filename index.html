UNIT 01 — ADAPTIVE-SMC-4DOF SIM FEED
Nonlinear Adaptive Control

Adaptive Sliding-Mode Control of a 4-DOF Manipulator

A fully nonlinear adaptive sliding-mode controller performing multi-target pick-and-place inside a constrained workspace — with link masses underestimated by 20% and payload mass by 57%. The controller estimates parameters online and stays stable through instantaneous payload changes and wall-impact contact dynamics.

UNIT 02 — BALL-BEAM-PID HW FEED
Embedded Hardware · Real-Time Control

Ball-and-Beam PID Controller

A physical real-time control system designed and built from scratch: a Time-of-Flight sensor measures ball position, a PID loop computes the correction, and a servo tilts the beam to hold the setpoint. Gains are tuned live from an onboard joystick with an OLED dashboard — no computer required.

UNIT 03 — IMPACT-SIM SIM FEED
Physics Simulation

Mass-Dependent Bouncing Ball Physics Simulator

An impulse-based physics engine demonstrating why heavier objects don't always travel farther: friction forces scale with mass during collisions. Models restitution, Coulomb friction, sliding-vs-sticking detection, and per-impact energy loss across 12+ bounces.

UNIT 04 — SMC-3LINK TRAJECTORY TRACKING

3-Link Robot Artist — Sliding-Mode Trajectory Tracking

A 3-DOF planar manipulator with fully symbolic Lagrangian dynamics tracks a cursive handwriting trajectory under model uncertainty — steady-state error under 0.01 rad, chattering eliminated via boundary-layer saturation.

UNIT 05 — ROT-PROJECTILE RIGID-BODY KINEMATICS

Rotating Link Projectile — Tip Impact Detection

A rigid rod launched with coupled linear and angular velocity: the simulation solves which tip strikes the ground first, where it lands, and the complete kinematic state at impact — a hybrid kinematics and root-finding problem.

Nonlinear Adaptive Control

Adaptive Sliding-Mode Control of a 4-DOF Manipulator

A fully nonlinear adaptive sliding-mode controller performing multi-target pick-and-place inside a constrained workspace — with link masses underestimated by 20% and payload mass underestimated by 57%. The controller adapts online and stays stable through instantaneous payload changes and wall-impact contact dynamics.

Embedded Hardware · Real-Time Control

Ball-and-Beam PID Controller

A physical real-time control system I designed and built: a Time-of-Flight sensor measures ball position at ~28.6 Hz, a PID loop computes the correction, and a servo tilts the beam to hold the setpoint. Gains are tuned live from an onboard joystick with an OLED dashboard — no computer required.

Physics Simulation

Mass-Dependent Bouncing Ball Physics Simulator

An impulse-based physics engine demonstrating why heavier objects don't always travel farther: friction forces scale with mass during collisions. Models coefficient of restitution, Coulomb friction, sliding-vs-sticking detection, and per-impact energy loss across 12+ bounces.
